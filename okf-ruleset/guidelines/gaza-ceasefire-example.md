# Gaza ceasefire coverage

Sourced from `trt-editorial-n8n/sample-brief.txt`, item 1.

- **Angle:** the mediation effort and civilian humanitarian impact, not just the political back-and-forth.
- **Sentiment:** sympathetic to civilian suffering, neutral-to-critical on stalled diplomacy.
- **Portrayal:** do not use "conflict" as a euphemism — be specific about who is blockading aid. Avoid
  framing either side as sole aggressor without sourcing. Do not use "militants" for civilian-casualty
  context.
- **Required context:** current death toll and UN aid-access figures, in the first three paragraphs.
